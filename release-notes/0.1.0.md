# TotK Recomp Multiplayer 0.1.0 (TotK Recomp 0.3.13)

The first release of online play for TotK Recomp: a server you host yourself, and a game that joins it. This release is
about being together. You see who is on, talk to them and jump to them. There is no shared world yet.

## What is in it

- **Chat.** Press Enter to type, Enter to send. Lines fade out a few seconds after the last one.
- **Player list.** Press P to see who is on the server: name, area, team and ping, with HOST and MOD badges.
- **Wheel menu.** Hold V (keyboard) or Minus + left stick click (controller), aim, let go to choose:
  - *Teleport* is coming in a later version: it is hidden in this one.
  - *Chat*: open the chat.
  - *Team*: make a team, invite players, join or leave one.
  - *Host tools*: for the host and moderators (summon all, kick, mute, ban, message, give or take moderator).
- **Online tab** in the settings menu (F1): your name, the server address, connect or leave, and the keys.
- **Server.** One small program for Windows and Linux. No game files, no account anywhere. Settings, ranks, bans and
  passwords are in [docs/server.md](../docs/server.md).

## What you need

- A legal copy of *Tears of the Kingdom* (base game and the 1.1.0, 1.2.1 or 1.4.3 update) and your own `prod.keys`,
  as for TotK Recomp. The setup builds the game on your PC from your files; nothing of the game is in these downloads.
- The same PC requirements as TotK Recomp: Windows 10 or later or Linux, a GPU with Vulkan 1.3, about 36 GiB free during
  the setup. See the [README](../README.md).
- A server to join, run by you or a friend.

## Play

1. Download `TotKMP-Setup-0.1.0-windows.zip` (Windows) or `TotKMP-Setup-0.1.0-x86_64.AppImage` (Linux). Unpack,
   start the setup, pick your files, and, if you know it, fill in *Server address*. The setup uses its own folder
   and never touches an existing TotK Recomp install.
2. Start the game. If you gave an address it connects by itself; otherwise open the Online tab (F1), type
   `host:port` and press Connect.

## Host

1. Download `TotKMP-Server-0.1.0-windows.zip` or `TotKMP-Server-0.1.0-linux.tar.gz` and unpack it.
2. Run `start-server.bat` (Windows) or `./start-server.sh` (Linux). The first start makes the server's key and prints
   the address and port.
3. Open TCP port 27120 on your firewall and router for players outside your network, and give them
   `your-address:27120`. Friends on the same router also need `max_per_ip` raised in `server-data/server.toml`.

The manual is `README.md` in the server download.

## Known limits

- **Teleport is not in this release.** It does not move the player yet; it comes in a later version.
- **No world sync yet.** You do not see other players' bodies, and enemies, items and the weather are not shared.
  Other players show only in the player list (area, team, ping).
- The setup and the exes are unsigned; SmartScreen warns on first start.
- Up to 32 players per server, 8 by default.
- The game window's title still says "TotK Recomp".
